**Rationale:**
Accurate, safe, click-free tone generation is the core product capability.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. The engine produces the requested equal-tempered frequency using configurable concert A.
2. Starting, stopping, and changing pitch are ramped to avoid clicks.
3. Playback never starts implicitly and responds predictably to interruption and route changes.
4. Frequency math and engine state behavior have focused automated tests.

**Test Steps:**
- Verify calculated frequencies against known note values.
- Inspect generated buffers for continuity and bounded amplitude.
- Perform physical-device listening checks before verification.
