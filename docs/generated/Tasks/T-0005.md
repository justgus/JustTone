**Rationale:**
Watch platform constraints may materially change the intended companion interaction.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. The Watch generates reference audio locally and never acts as an implicit remote playback trigger.
2. The companion exposes the approved profile, pitch, timbre, level, route, playback, and resume flow.
3. Profile data transferred to Watch is versioned, atomic, and remains usable while the iPhone is unreachable.
4. Simulator logic tests and required physical-device Watch qualification remain clearly separated.
